Welcome to ScoobyAAC

Adaptive AAC • Fast Composition • Partner Mirroring

ScoobyAAC is an adaptive, high-contrast Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) platform engineered for motor accessibility, rapid composition, and clear conversation partner collaboration.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

1

Apple Keyboard & Swipe

QuickPath

Full Apple mobile layout (ABC, 123, #+=) with continuous swipe-to-type (glide path). Type on physical keyboard or tap 🗗 Undock for the full on-screen keyboard.

2

Speak Sentence

Tap the message window or press 🔊 Speak (or Space/Enter) to vocalize with live word highlighting.

3

180° Partner Flip

Sitting across a table? Tap 🔄 Flip (Alt+F) to invert the message window upright for your partner.

4

Phone Mirroring

Tap 📱 Partner (Alt+Q) for a QR code. Partners can mirror text & send reaction emojis (👍, ⏳, 🛑) from their phone!

5

Gesture Recognition

Hands-Free

Supports Movesense 9-DoF BLE & MediaPipe Vision tracking. Pinch speaks the active message, while hand poses trigger mapped activity phrases!

6

Sequential Shortcuts

Single-finger friendly: tap ` for Leader Mode (P Speak, C Clear, F Flip), tap Ctrl to latch Sticky Keys, or press F1–F4 for instant word predictions.
